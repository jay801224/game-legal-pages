NyanVeil Android — 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-10-19

Scope and contact | 適用範圍與聯絡

本說明適用於 NyanVeil Android 版（包括 1.1.0）。功能會因帳號、服務狀態及版本而不同。開發者聯絡名稱為 DoraCmeow，電子郵件為 fattyjay1224@gmail.com。本 Android 說明不涵蓋 Apple CloudKit 或 Game Center。

This notice describes NyanVeil for Android, including version 1.1.0. Features may depend on the account, service availability and app version. The developer contact is DoraCmeow, fattyjay1224@gmail.com. This Android notice does not describe Apple CloudKit or Game Center.

Accounts and game data | 帳號與遊戲資料

我們處理訪客或帳號識別碼，用來關聯遊戲進度、安全及購買紀錄。選擇 Google 或 App 提供的其他登入方式時，會處理該服務提供的帳號識別碼，以及提供的名稱或電子郵件。遊戲資料包括貓咪、房間佈置、進度、偏好設定，以及你選擇提交的社交或排行榜資料。

We process a guest/account identifier to associate progress, security and purchase records. If you choose Google sign-in or another offered sign-in provider, we process the account identifier and any name or email supplied by that service. Game data includes cats, room decoration, progress, preferences and any social or leaderboard information you choose to submit.

Local and cloud storage | 本機與雲端儲存

進度會儲存在裝置上。可使用雲端備份時，初始備份偏好為開啟；既有存檔可能保留先前選擇。設定中提供的控制項可調整這項偏好。帳號、購買驗證、安全及刪除紀錄的處理，不受遊戲備份開關影響。登入及雲端服務使用 Google Firebase；傳送至我們服務的資料使用加密連線。

Progress is stored on the device. Where cloud backup is available, the initial backup preference is enabled; an existing save can retain your earlier choice. Available Settings controls let you change that preference. Account, purchase-verification, security and deletion records are processed independently of game-backup settings. Google Firebase supports authentication and cloud services. Data sent to our services uses encrypted connections.

Purchases | 購買

付款由 Google Play 處理。我們處理商品識別碼、購買驗證資料、帳號綁定識別碼及已發放的遊戲權益，用來確認所有權、還原購買及避免重複發放。我們不會取得完整付款卡號。舊紀錄可能沒有原始成交金額或日期，不會使用目前售價補造缺少的交易資料。Google 自行管理其訂單紀錄。

Google Play handles payment. We process product identifiers, purchase-verification data, an account-bound identifier and awarded game entitlements to verify ownership, restore purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your full payment card number. Historical rows may lack an original paid amount or date; current prices are not used to invent missing transaction metadata. Google separately controls its own order records.

Rewarded ads and privacy choices | 獎勵廣告與隱私選擇

可使用獎勵廣告時，會透過 Google AdMob 提供自願觀看的廣告。Android 1.1.0 會要求非個人化廣告，並先檢查同意資格；適用的同意表單與隱私選項依地區及服務設定而定，無法確認資格時會阻擋請求。非個人化不表示完全不處理資料：廣告服務可能收集及分享 IP 位址、由 IP 推得的大致位置、裝置或廣告識別碼、App 或廣告互動及診斷資料，用於廣告、衡量及防詐。Android App 不要求 GPS 精確或粗略定位權限。

When available, optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob. Android 1.1.0 requests non-personalized ads and checks consent eligibility before requesting an ad. Applicable consent forms and privacy options depend on region and service configuration; if eligibility cannot be established, the request is blocked. Non-personalized does not mean no data processing: the advertising service may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The Android app does not request precise or coarse GPS location permission.

Security, retention and deletion | 安全、保留與刪除

雲端服務會處理服務請求及錯誤資訊，以維持運作與安全。帳號及遊戲資料會在提供帳號與服務所需期間保留，並依刪除要求處理。刪除完成後，會移除原始 Firebase 帳號 UID、其直接帳號關聯及原始購買憑證。為防止交易或帳號重複使用，以及記錄刪除完成狀態，仍可能保留最小化的化名紀錄：帳號綁定識別碼、由購買憑證衍生的雜湊，以及刪除要求識別碼、驗證值與版本。這不代表所有識別碼都已清除，也不表示剩餘紀錄完全匿名；這些最小化紀錄目前沒有固定到期日。Google 可能依自己的政策另外保留資料。可用的申請方式及保留限制，請見刪除帳號說明。

Our cloud services process operational request and error information to operate and protect the service. Account and game records remain while needed to provide the account and services, subject to deletion requests. After a completed deletion, the raw Firebase account UID, its direct account links and original purchase tokens are removed. Minimal pseudonymous records can remain to prevent transaction/account replay and record completed deletion: an account-bound identifier, receipt-derived hashes, and deletion-request identifiers, verification values and revisions. These are not a claim that all identifiers have been erased or that the remaining records are fully anonymous. No fixed expiry is currently specified for these minimized records. Google may independently retain data under its own policies. See the account deletion instructions for the available request routes and retained-data limits.

Audience and your choices | 適用年齡與選擇

Android 版適用於 13 歲及以上使用者，不以未滿 13 歲兒童為對象。若兒童提供了個人資料，或你要詢問存取、更正或刪除資料，請聯絡我們。你可以不使用選擇性的登入或獎勵廣告、調整可用的備份選項，以及變更 Android 廣告識別碼設定；使用帳號或購買服務時，仍需要相應的帳號及安全處理。

The Android app is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if a child has provided personal information or if you want to ask about access, correction or deletion. You can decline optional sign-in and rewarded ads, use available backup controls, and adjust Android advertising-ID settings. Some account or purchase-security processing is necessary for those services.
