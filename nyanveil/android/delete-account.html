NyanVeil Android — 刪除帳號 / Account Deletion

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-10-19

若 App 內的刪除服務尚未開放或無法使用，請使用下方電子郵件方式。

If in-app deletion is unavailable, use the email route below. An unavailable button or pending request is not a completed deletion.

提出要求 / Request deletion

在可用的 Android 版本及服務設定下，可由「設定 → 刪除帳號」提出要求。請閱讀確認內容；系統可能要求重新驗證。只有確認完成的要求才會開始刪除；準備或待處理狀態不代表刪除完成。

Where supported and enabled, use Settings → Delete Account, review the confirmation and complete any required authentication. Only a committed request begins deletion. Prepared or pending status does not mean deletion is complete.

Email fattyjay1224@gmail.com with subject “NyanVeil Android account deletion” and your game account ID if available. If you cannot obtain the ID, describe the sign-in method you used. We may request appropriate proof of account ownership. Do not send passwords, full card details or original purchase tokens.

刪除及保留資料 / Removed and retained data

刪除完成後，會移除 Firebase 帳號、雲端遊戲資料、原始 Firebase 帳號 UID 的直接關聯，以及原始購買憑證。為防止交易或帳號重複使用，仍可能保留帳號綁定識別碼與憑證衍生雜湊等最小化的化名紀錄；另會保留刪除要求識別碼、驗證值、完成狀態與版本，其 Firebase UID 欄位會清空。這些紀錄並非完全匿名，且目前沒有固定到期日。尚在處理的交易、安全隔離或需要人工檢查的資料，可能讓要求保持待處理狀態；不會在完成清理前宣稱完成。

Completed deletion removes the Firebase account, cloud game data, direct links to the raw Firebase account UID and original purchase tokens. Minimal pseudonymous anti-replay records can remain, including an account-bound identifier and receipt-derived hashes. Deletion-completion records retain request identifiers, verification values, completion state and revisions, with the Firebase UID field cleared. These records are not fully anonymous and currently have no fixed expiry. Outstanding transactions, safety fences or data requiring operator review can keep a request pending; completion is not claimed before cleanup finishes.

Google Play 自行保存的訂單不會因此刪除。刪除帳號可能使進度及已發放權益無法再取得，並不自動取消 Google Play 訂單或核發退款；相關事項由 Google Play 處理。

This does not erase orders independently held by Google Play. Account deletion can make progress and awarded rights inaccessible. It does not automatically cancel Play orders or issue refunds; Google Play handles those requests.
