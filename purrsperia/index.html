Purrsperia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Purrsperia ("the App") is a single-player offline cat-magic action game. We built it to collect as little data as possible. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what is and isn't collected.

Purrsperia(「本應用程式」)是一款單機離線的貓咪魔法動作遊戲。我們的設計原則是盡量少收集資料。本隱私權政策清楚說明哪些資料會、哪些不會被收集。

1. The short version | 重點摘要

Purrsperia has no user accounts, no login, and no servers. Your game progress and settings stay only on your device. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or photos, and we do not track you across other apps (we do not request the iOS App Tracking Transparency permission and do not use your advertising identifier for tracking).

Purrsperia 沒有帳號、不需登入、沒有伺服器。你的遊戲進度與設定只存在你的裝置上。我們不收集你的姓名、電子郵件、位置、通訊錄或相片,也不會跨其他 App 追蹤你(我們不要求 iOS 追蹤許可,也不使用廣告識別碼進行追蹤)。

2. Data processed by third-party SDKs | 第三方 SDK 處理的資料

The only data leaving your device is handled by two third-party services:

ServiceDataPurpose
Google AdMob
廣告		 Device identifiers and ad-interaction data, used to serve interstitial ads. We use non-personalized ads only. Display ads to keep the game free. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
Sentry
當機/錯誤診斷		 Crash and error diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version, and error messages. Personal file paths, emails, and secret-shaped strings are automatically redacted before sending. Find and fix crashes and bugs. Governed by Sentry's Privacy Policy.

唯一會離開你裝置的資料,由上述兩個第三方服務處理:Google AdMob(投放非個人化插頁廣告)與 Sentry(當機/錯誤診斷,送出前會自動遮蔽個人路徑、電子郵件與疑似密鑰字串)。

3. Data stored only on your device | 僅存於你裝置上的資料

Game progress, Moonlight, cosmetics, high scores, and settings are saved locally on your device only (in the app's local storage). We have no servers and cannot see or access this data. Deleting the App removes it.

遊戲進度、月光幣、外觀、最高分與設定僅儲存在你裝置的本機儲存空間。我們沒有伺服器,無法看到或存取這些資料。刪除 App 即會移除這些資料。

4. In-app purchases | 應用程式內購買

The current version contains no real-money in-app purchases. All in-game currency and cosmetics are earned through play. If purchases are added in the future, this policy will be updated and any payment will be processed by Apple — we never receive your card details.

目前版本沒有任何真實付費的應用程式內購買。所有遊戲內貨幣與外觀皆透過遊玩取得。若日後加入購買功能,將更新本政策,且任何付款均由 Apple 處理,我們不會取得你的卡片資料。

5. Children | 兒童

Purrsperia is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Purrsperia 為一般年齡層遊戲,並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計。我們不會在知情下收集兒童的個人資訊。

6. Your choices | 你的選擇

You can reset all local data by deleting and reinstalling the App. You can limit ad personalization in your device's settings. Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is no account to delete — see our Data Deletion page.

你可透過刪除並重新安裝 App 來清除所有本機資料,並可在裝置設定中限制廣告個人化。由於我們的伺服器不持有任何個人資料,因此沒有帳號需要刪除 — 詳見資料刪除頁面。

7. Changes & contact | 變更與聯絡

We may update this policy as the App evolves; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly. Questions? Contact fattyjay1224@gmail.com.

我們可能隨 App 演進更新本政策,「最後更新」日期會隨之變更。有任何問題請來信 fattyjay1224@gmail.com。
