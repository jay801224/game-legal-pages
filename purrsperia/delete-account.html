Purrsperia — Data Deletion

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Purrsperia has no user accounts and stores no personal data on any server. There is no account to delete and nothing for us to erase on our side — we hold none of your data.

Purrsperia 沒有使用者帳號,也不在任何伺服器上儲存個人資料。因此沒有帳號需要刪除,我們這邊也沒有任何你的資料需要清除 — 我們不持有你的任何資料。

1. Delete your local game data | 刪除你的本機遊戲資料

All your progress, Moonlight, cosmetics, scores, and settings are stored only on your device. To delete all of it, simply delete the App from your device (press and hold the app icon → Remove App → Delete App). Reinstalling gives you a fresh start with no previous data.

你所有的進度、月光幣、外觀、分數與設定僅儲存於你的裝置上。要全部刪除,只需從裝置移除本 App(長按 App 圖示 → 移除 App → 刪除 App)。重新安裝即為全新開始,不保留任何先前資料。

2. Ad & diagnostics data | 廣告與診斷資料

Ad data is handled by Google and diagnostics by Sentry, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising identifier and limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising).

廣告資料由 Google 處理、診斷資料由 Sentry 處理,詳見隱私權政策。你可在裝置設定中重設廣告識別碼並限制廣告個人化(設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤 / Apple 廣告)。

3. Need help? | 需要協助?

If you have any questions about data deletion, email fattyjay1224@gmail.com and we'll respond as soon as we can.

若你對資料刪除有任何疑問,請來信 fattyjay1224@gmail.com,我們會盡快回覆。
